A feel for the invisible.

AirLoop quietly measures carbon dioxide, fine particles, temperature, and humidity. Its wall sensor tracks subtle changes throughout the day, giving the system a clearer picture of your room’s conditions without a camera, a microphone, or another demand on your attention.

Four signals. A clearer picture.

At home with your rhythm.

Over time, AirLoop’s AI learns recurring patterns in your home’s air, from evening study sessions to morning showers. Routine decisions stay on the device whenever possible, helping the system anticipate familiar changes while you choose what it remembers and shares.

Routine learning. Local decisions whenever possible.

Awareness becomes action.

When conditions change, AirLoop adjusts ventilation or coordinates compatible purifiers and dehumidifiers within your chosen settings. It considers outdoor conditions before bringing air inside, then eases back as the room settles. A soft light offers reassurance without interrupting your day.

Ventilate. Dehumidify. Purify. Then ease back.
